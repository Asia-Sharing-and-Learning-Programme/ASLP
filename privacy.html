Privacy

The Asia-Pacific Sharing & Learning Programme (ASLP) website is run by The Salvation Army for its Asia-Pacific community development network.

What we keep

Signing in uses your email address only, to check which pages you may edit. The website records when sign-in emails are sent and when people sign in, to keep the service secure. Names, roles and work emails shown on the site are entered by ASLP administrators and Cluster Working Group leads.

Files you upload

Updates, newsletters, PDFs, PowerPoints and photos posted through the console are published on this website. Large files are stored in the ASLP's Google Drive and shared with a link so visitors can download them. The website can only reach files it uploaded itself; it does not read any other files in that Drive.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share personal information, show advertising or use tracking cookies.

Questions

To ask about or remove information about you, contact the ASLP through your Cluster Working Group lead or The Salvation Army Switzerland's International Development team.
